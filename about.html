About Us

Built for the trade,
not the desk.

TradeFlow AI exists because the best tradespeople in the UK deserve better tools — ones that work as hard as they do.

Why we built this

Too many skilled tradespeople lose jobs because a quote took too long. Too many work hard all month and then spend evenings chasing invoices. Too many lose thousands to clients who just don't pay.

The tools that exist were built for accountants and office workers — not someone who's been on a roof since 7am and needs to send a professional quote from their van at lunchtime.

TradeFlow AI was built to change that. Fast quoting, automatic invoice chasing, Stripe-powered payments — all from your phone, in seconds.

Our mission

To give every UK tradesperson the technology to run their business like a pro — so they can spend more time on the tools and less time on the paperwork.
