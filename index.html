FAQ

Questions answered.

How does the AI know what to charge for my trade? ▼

TradeFlow AI is trained on real UK trade pricing data, adjusted by trade type, region, and job complexity. You can always review and edit any figure before sending — you're always in control.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use it? ▼

Not at all. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use TradeFlow AI. It's designed specifically for tradespeople, not accountants or developers.

Can I use my own branding on quotes? ▼

Yes — add your logo, business name, and contact details. Every quote goes out branded as your business, not TradeFlow AI.

How does the automatic chasing work? ▼

When you send a quote or invoice, TradeFlow AI follows up with professional, polite emails at set intervals. You can customise timing and switch off reminders for individual clients.

Is the 14-day trial really free? ▼

Completely free. No credit card required. Full access to every feature for 14 days. At the end, you choose a plan or your account pauses — nothing is charged automatically.

What happens to my data if I cancel? ▼

Your data is yours. Export all quotes, invoices, and client records at any time. If you cancel, we keep your data for 90 days before permanently deleting it.
